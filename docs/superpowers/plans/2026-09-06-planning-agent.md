- **Exactly one commit per task**, subject byte-identical to the section's `**commit subject:**`, both trailers in the WORKSPACE RULES order. Shell is `set -euo pipefail`, shellcheck-clean; `shfmt -i 2 -ci` formats `*.sh` and the two hooks.
