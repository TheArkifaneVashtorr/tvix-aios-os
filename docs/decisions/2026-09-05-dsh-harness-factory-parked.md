1. **Parked:** `~/flakes/dsh-harness/factory/dark-factory.js` — the multi-model planning→build factory (DeepSeek architecture / Kimi product / GLM feasibility lenses, consolidation, build, cross-review) and its role→model map (`deepseekPro`, `deepseekFlash`, `kimi`, `glm`, `glmFlash`).
