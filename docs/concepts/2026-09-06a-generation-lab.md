# Concept 2026-09-06a — the generation lab: thousands of rounds, graded, then entered

**Class:** product / media. **Status:** idea (operator, 2026-09-06 ~00:50 CDT; brainstorm owed before a spec). **Origin:** the operator's words: "the model could just get loaded and unloaded when needed. Run a few thousand prompt iterations, run a few thousand generations, grade, review, analyze the data. I want to compete in some of the competitions on CivitAI." Sits on the ComfyUI worlds (spec 2026-09-05, sub-project 2: the job queue on the API, the feed, learning from likes) and on the telemetry store (spec 2026-09-05: numbers, never content, unless the operator says otherwise for this lab).

**Idea.** A lane job that owns ComfyUI's API for a night: it loads a model set, runs a few thousand prompt variations (mutations of a seed prompt, LoRA strengths, samplers, steps, seeds), renders, unloads the diffusion model, loads a local vision-language judge, grades every image on prompt adherence and a small aesthetic score, keeps the best, breeds the next round, and writes every round's parameters and scores to a table. In the morning the operator reviews the top set in the feed and marks picks; the picks are the ground truth that recalibrates the judge. Loading and unloading is what makes it fit one 32 GB card: Krea 2 in fp8 plus its text encoder take about 21 GB, a 4B to 8B vision judge in 4-bit about 6 GB, so they never share the card — the API frees one before the other loads, at a cost of seconds per round. Renders take 5 to 6 seconds at 8 steps, so a night is thousands of rounds with judging in the loop.

**Payoff.** A competition entry is the best of a searched space rather than the best of a handful of hand tries; the same table answers which LoRAs, strengths and samplers actually move the operator's picks; the judge model, the feed and the store are the sub-projects the worlds plan already owes, so the lab is mostly wiring.

**Dependencies.** The worlds' wave 2 and 3 (W3b, W5b, W4, W6, WH1: the units, the feed, the drill); a judge model chosen and packaged (Qwen3-VL class, 4-bit; the text encoder Krea 2 already uses is that family); the telemetry store's `host-metrics` and a lab stream for rounds (parameters and scores are numbers; prompts are content — the operator decides whether the lab's own table may hold them, on the machine only); a download-only allowlist for civitai.com through the broker if models or contest assets are to be fetched by a lane rather than by hand; the operator's brainstorm answers below.

**Earliest landing.** After the worlds land: one brainstorm, then one plan of three or four tasks (the loop as a lane job; the judge package; the lab table and the morning review in the feed; the analysis report). Entering a competition stays the operator's own act: the harness never uploads anything anywhere.

**Brainstorm questions for the operator** (each with a recommendation): which competitions and by when — their themes and rules set the model set and the schedule (recommended: pick one with a month's horizon); what "grade" is — prompt adherence by the judge, aesthetics by a small scorer, the operator's picks as truth (recommended: all three, picks override); whether training or merging LoRAs is in scope or only searching over the ones on disk (recommended: search first, training later); which world runs it (recommended: the SFW world for competition work; the NSFW world only by explicit choice); what the lab table may hold (recommended: parameters, scores and image hashes always; prompt text only inside the world's own lab repository, never in the evidence store); how contest assets and new models arrive (recommended: by hand for now; a download-only broker allowlist later).
